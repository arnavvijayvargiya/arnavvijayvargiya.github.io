A Life in Perpetual Motion

Rooted in the ancient soil of भारतम्, I find myself perpetually drawn to the intersection of tradition and modernity—where centuries-old wisdom meets contemporary curiosity.

My days unfold through storytelling, meandering walks with no particular destination, the occasional necessity of code, and the gentle art of transforming life's beautiful chaos into something worth sharing.

Present Pursuits

A glimpse into current endeavors — Last updated July 19, 2025

Nestled in Jaipur's embrace, where the summer sun writes poetry in degrees celsius, I find myself in that curious state between productivity and contemplation.

My evenings are devoted to what I diplomatically call "cinematic research"—a careful curation of films and stories that speak to the human condition. Meanwhile, my kitchen serves as an experimental theater where rotis transform into abstract art, each charred edge a testament to my creative process.

Books accumulate around me like faithful companions, their spines promising future conversations. I collect them with the optimism of someone who believes time expands to accommodate all worthy pursuits.

Dreams of cooler climates dance in my mind—perhaps the ethereal landscapes of Leh, the serene valleys of Kashmir, or in moments of particular candor, simply the reliable sanctuary of an open refrigerator door.

This page draws inspiration from Derek Sivers' Now page movement—a beautiful practice of sharing one's current focus with the world.
